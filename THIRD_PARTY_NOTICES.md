# Third-party material and research provenance

The MIT license applies only to original contributions. It does not relicense third-party
material or establish redistribution permission where no upstream license is documented.

- `main.py` and its dated variants derive from Kaito Fukami's public V36 robust-hybrid
  Kaggriculture agent. The README records source and variant hashes and the horizon change.
- `experiments/candidates/` contains public Kaggle agent and notebook extracts, including
  embedded/decoded payloads. Candidate README files, metadata, scripts and research logs
  record author handles, notebook slugs and extraction details. These remain upstream works.
- Existing nested `LICENSE.txt` and `NOTICE.txt` files are retained and apply to their
  corresponding upstream material. A public notebook alone is not evidence of an MIT license.
- Third-party agents or extracts without a verified license are research references with
  unverified licensing status; no additional permission is granted by this repository.
- The official game engine is installed as `kaggle-environments`; downloaded competition
  files, virtual environments and generated replays are excluded from this repository.

Competition: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/kaggriculture

Local benchmark outcomes and dated API snapshots are not final leaderboard certification.
